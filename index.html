About

Engineer, builder and community leader

I am a Software Engineer in the AI Core Team at Samsung Electronics (South Korea), working on the core AI technology behind Samsung products and services. Previously I was part of the SmartHome AI Lab, building AI for millions of connected homes, and before that I spent four years in Samsung’s Networks business — first in cloud platform & infrastructure R&D, then in strategic partnership management and business strategy.

I graduated in Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) from the Indian Institute of Technology Delhi. I am a Certified Kubernetes Administrator (CKA) and an active open-source contributor — my cloud-native networking work runs in production Kubernetes clusters around the world. My interests span artificial intelligence, machine learning, cloud infrastructure, Kubernetes networking and distributed systems.

Beyond engineering, I lead the Indian community in Korea as President of the IIT Alumni Association of Korea (IITAAK), Core Team Member of Indians in Korea (IIK) and Executive Secretary of Indian Students and Researchers in Korea (ISRK). I love learning something new every single day — and above all, I love to code.
