About

Engineer, builder and community leader

I am a Software Engineer in the SmartHome AI Lab (AI Core Team) at Samsung Electronics (South Korea), where I work on artificial intelligence for the connected home. Before that I spent four years in Samsung’s Networks business, building cloud platform & infrastructure and later shaping solution architecture and strategic partnerships as a business manager.

I graduated in Computer Science and Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology Delhi (B.Tech 2020, CGPA 9.04). My interests span AI & machine learning, cloud infrastructure, Kubernetes networking, distributed systems and building web and mobile products end to end.

Outside work I serve as President of the IIT Alumni Association of Korea (IITAAK), connecting IITians across Korea and building bridges between the Indian and Korean tech communities. I love learning something new every single day — and above all, I love to code.
